# 3EAL — Official Game Rules

## 1. Game Objective

Be the first player to form **3 complete sets of 3 cards** (9 cards total matching valid patterns) to claim victory.

---

## 2. Setup

1. Each player is dealt **3 Normal Cards** and **0 Action Cards** at the start of the game.
2. The remaining cards form the draw deck in the center.
3. First player is randomly selected as the starting active player.

---

## 3. Valid Set Patterns

A set consists of 3 cards that satisfy **any one** of these 4 criteria:

* **Same Color:** 3 cards of color `C0C0FF` (Periwinkle), `008080` (Teal), or `C06060` (Rose)
* **Same Number:** 3 cards sharing the same digit (1 to 7)
* **Same Shape:** 3 cards sharing the same shape (Circle, Triangle, Square, Pentagon, or Hexagon)
* **Consecutive Numbers:** 3 cards with sequential digits (e.g., 1-2-3, 4-5-6)

### Special: TEAL Wild Cards
TEAL cards (color `008080`) are wild cards that can adopt **any shape and any number** to complete a set pattern. Their color is always fixed as Teal.

---

## 4. Turn Flow

On your turn, execute the following phases in order:

### 4.1 Draw Phase
Draw **1 card** from the deck. If the deck is empty, the discard pile is shuffled to form a new deck.

### 4.2 Main Phase
You may perform the following actions **in any order, unlimited times**:

* **Discard Normal Cards:** Place any number of normal cards from your hand onto the discard pile.
* **Play Action Cards:** Use action cards from your hand:
  * **CONCEAL** — Target one of your own revealed cards to hide it from opponents again.
  * **STEAL** — Take a normal card (including TEAL) from an opponent's hand. Cannot target action cards or completed sets.
  * **REVEAL** — Force an opponent to reveal a card from their hand to all players.
  * **TEAL** — Not "played" as an action; stays in hand as a wild normal card.
* **Interrupt Window:** When you play CONCEAL, STEAL, or REVEAL targeting an opponent, the game enters the **Interrupt Phase**.

### 4.3 Interrupt Phase (Triggered by Targeted Actions)
When targeted by CONCEAL, STEAL, or REVEAL, the targeted player has **10 seconds** to respond:

* **Play APPEAL** — If the targeted player has an APPEAL card in hand, they may play it to block the action. Both the original action card and the APPEAL card are sent to the discard pile. The action is cancelled.
* **Do Nothing** — If no APPEAL is played within 10 seconds, the action resolves normally.

### 4.4 End Phase
1. **Hand Size Limit:** You must discard down until you have a **maximum of 9 cards** in your hand.
2. **Win Check:** If you now have 3 valid sets of 3 cards (9 cards total), you win immediately!
3. Turn passes to the next player, who begins their Draw Phase.

---

## 5. Action Cards

| Card | Type | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| **CONCEAL** | Action | Target your own revealed card to hide it from opponents again. Can be appealed. |
| **STEAL** | Action | Take a normal card (including TEAL) from an opponent's hand. Cannot target action cards or completed sets. Can be appealed. |
| **REVEAL** | Action | Force an opponent to reveal a card from their hand to all players. Can be appealed. |
| **APPEAL** | Action | Play during an interrupt window to block an opponent's CONCEAL, STEAL, or REVEAL action. Both action cards are discarded. |
| **TEAL** | Wild Normal | A wild card with fixed Teal color (#008080) but flexible shape and number. Can be used to complete any set pattern. Counts as a normal card for targeting (can be stolen, revealed, concealed). |

---

## 6. Deck Composition

* **105 Normal Cards:** 3 colors × 7 numbers × 5 shapes = 105 unique combinations
* **15 Action Cards:** 3 copies each of CONCEAL, STEAL, REVEAL, APPEAL, TEAL
* **Total:** 120 cards

---

## 7. Winning the Game

The game ends immediately when any player forms **3 valid sets of 3 cards** (9 cards). The player who achieves this wins.

A valid set = 3 cards matching **one** of the 4 patterns listed in Section 3.
TEAL wild cards can fill any role needed for a pattern.

---

## 8. Additional Notes

* **Revealed Cards:** Cards can be in a "revealed" state (visible to all players) or "concealed" (hidden, only visible to owner). REVEAL action exposes a card; CONCEAL hides it again.
* **Discard Pile:** All discarded and played action cards go here. Used to reshuffle when deck empties.
* **Action Card Limits:** No limit on how many action cards you can play in a single turn during Main Phase.
* **Targeting Restrictions:** STEAL cannot target action cards (except TEAL) or cards already in completed sets.
